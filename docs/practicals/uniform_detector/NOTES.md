## Results observed (CPU, seed 0)

| run | model | epochs | lr | params | seconds | test acc |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| first model | 1 conv (4 kernels), pool 8 | 5 | 0.001 | 369 | 0.5–1.7 | 0.72 |
| more epochs | same | 15 | 0.001 | 369 | 1.3 | 0.81 |
| larger lr | same | 15 | 0.01 | 369 | 1.3 | 0.99 (seed 1: 0.99) |
| two conv blocks | 8 + 16 kernels | 15 | 0.001 | 2,417 | 2.7 | 0.97 (seed 1: 0.76) |
| two conv blocks | same | 15 | 0.01 | 2,417 | 2.9 | 0.98 |
| two conv blocks | same | 15 | 0.1 | 2,417 | 2.5 | 0.50 (stuck) |
| 1 × 1 kernels | Advanced 2 | 15 | 0.01 | 273 | 1.5 | 0.91 |

The task is easy for a CNN, so the challenge is about calibration and about reaching the target with few parameters. The smallest solution found has 369 parameters.
